# Privacy policy

This app is used by one person only: its owner. It requests read-only access to the
owner's own Google data (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Sheets, Docs, Contacts). Data is read
on the owner's own machine to prepare summaries for the owner. Nothing is sold, shared
with third parties, or used for advertising. Access can be revoked at any time at
https://myaccount.google.com/connections.
